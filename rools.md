# Mantouvert Website Design System & Creative Direction

### Version 1.0 (Strict Design Guide for Developers)

> **This document is the single source of truth. Every page, section, and component must follow these rules. Do not introduce styles that are not defined here. The website should feel like an editorial architecture magazine mixed with a premium construction company. Every pixel must communicate craftsmanship, luxury, and trust.**

---

# Design References

The website should follow the visual language shown in the references.

Characteristics:

• Huge typography

• Editorial layouts

• Large photography

• Lots of white space

• Strong grid system

• Sophisticated asymmetry

• Premium magazine feeling

• Minimal decorations

• Large image compositions

• Elegant spacing

• Calm colors

The site should never resemble a typical construction website.

Avoid:

❌ Hard shadows

❌ Bright gradients

❌ Rounded playful cards

❌ Cartoon icons

❌ Heavy borders

❌ Cheap stock design

❌ Generic templates

# Final Goal

When someone visits **Mantouvert**, they should immediately feel they are looking at a **high-end architecture and landscape studio**, not just a construction company. Every page should showcase exceptional craftsmanship through restrained design: generous whitespace, refined typography, immersive photography, and a calm, editorial aesthetic. The website should communicate confidence, precision, and timeless quality—making visitors believe that Mantouvert can design, build, repair, and maintain everything from elegant pools and cascading water features to gardens, villas, and complete outdoor living environments.
